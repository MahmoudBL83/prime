# 📸 Media Upload & Management - Quick Start Guide

## 🎯 How to Use the OnlyFans-Style Upload System

### 🚀 Step-by-Step Guide

---

## 1️⃣ Accessing the Upload Feature

### Option A: From Creator Dashboard
1. Go to **Mentors Page** (`/mentors`)
2. Click on **"Profile"** tab in navigation
3. Click **"Dashboard"** button (shows your creator dashboard)
4. Find the **"Content Management"** section
5. Click the **"Upload New Content"** button (purple-pink gradient)

### Option B: Quick Access
- Look for the bright **purple-pink gradient button** with an upload icon
- It says: "Upload New Content" (English) or "رفع محتوى جديد" (Arabic)

---

## 2️⃣ Step 1: Upload Your Media

### Drag & Drop Method:
1. Drag your files into the upload area
2. Files will appear as thumbnails below

### Click to Browse Method:
1. Click **"Choose Photos"** for images
2. Click **"Choose Videos"** for video files
3. Select multiple files from your device

### File Requirements:
- **Photos**: Up to 10MB each (JPG, PNG, GIF, WEBP)
- **Videos**: Up to 100MB each (MP4, MOV, AVI)
- **Multiple files**: Upload many at once!

### Preview Features:
- ✅ See thumbnails of all uploaded files
- ✅ View file sizes
- ✅ See video durations
- ✅ Remove individual files (trash icon on hover)
- ✅ Clear all files at once

---

## 3️⃣ Step 2: Add Details

### A. Write Your Caption
- Click in the text area
- Type up to 2000 characters
- Use the emoji picker (😀 button) for emojis
- Use @ for mentions
- Use # for hashtags

### B. Choose Access Level (WHO CAN SEE THIS?)

#### 🟢 Public
- **Free** for everyone
- Good for: Previews, teasers, promotional content
- Shows to: All visitors

#### 🔒 Basic Tier (49 EGP/month)
- For subscribers paying 49 EGP or more
- Good for: Regular exclusive content
- Shows to: Basic + Premium + VIP subscribers

#### ✨ Premium Tier (99 EGP/month)
- For subscribers paying 99 EGP or more
- Good for: High-quality exclusive content
- Shows to: Premium + VIP subscribers

#### 👑 VIP Tier (199 EGP/month)
- For subscribers paying 199 EGP or more
- Good for: Ultra-exclusive content
- Shows to: VIP subscribers only

#### 💰 Pay-Per-View (Custom Price)
- Set your own price (e.g., 29 EGP)
- One-time payment to unlock this specific post
- Good for: Special content, behind-the-scenes, exclusive sets
- Works for: Anyone willing to pay the price

**Tip**: Most creators use Basic for regular posts, Premium for weekly specials, and VIP for monthly exclusive content!

### C. Add Tags (Optional but Recommended!)
1. Type a tag in the input field
2. Click "Add" or press Enter
3. Tags help subscribers find your content
4. Examples: `education`, `tutorial`, `exclusive`, `premium`
5. Remove tags by clicking the ✕ on each tag

### D. Add Location (Optional)
- Add where the content was created
- Example: "Cairo, Egypt" or "My Studio"
- Helps subscribers connect with your content

---

## 4️⃣ Step 3: Schedule & Publish

### Post Now (Instant):
1. Select **"Post Now"** option
2. Click **"Post Now"** button
3. Your content goes live immediately!

### Schedule for Later:
1. Select **"Schedule"** option
2. Pick a **date** from the calendar
3. Choose a **time** (24-hour format)
4. Click **"Schedule Post"**
5. Post will be published automatically at that time!

**Scheduling Tips**:
- 📅 Best times: 7-9 PM (when subscribers are most active)
- 📊 Post consistently (daily or weekly schedule)
- ⏰ Use scheduling to maintain presence even when busy

### Review Before Posting:
Check the **Post Summary** at the bottom:
- Number of media files
- Access tier selected
- PPV price (if applicable)
- Number of tags
- Location (if added)

---

## 5️⃣ Managing Your Posts

### View All Your Content

#### Switch Between Tabs:
1. **All Posts**: See everything you've uploaded (156 posts)
2. **Photos**: Only images (124 photos)
3. **Videos**: Only video content (32 videos)
4. **Analytics**: View performance stats

#### Toggle View Mode:
- **Grid View** (□): Compact cards, great for browsing
- **List View** (☰): Detailed view with stats

### Edit or Delete Posts

#### Grid View:
1. Hover over any post
2. See engagement stats (likes, comments, views)
3. Click **"Edit"** to modify the post
4. Click **"Delete"** to remove it

#### List View:
- More detailed information
- Edit/Delete buttons on the right
- See post date and tier badge
- View engagement metrics

### Filter & Sort:
- Click **"Filter"** to show specific content types
- Click **"Sort"** to order by date/popularity

3. **VIP (199 EGP)**: Ultra-exclusive, monthly premieres
4. **PPV (Custom)**: Special sets, 1-on-1 content (29-99 EGP)

### Engagement Tips:

## 🔍 Troubleshooting

### "File too large" error:
- **Images**: Compress to under 10MB
- **Videos**: Use video compressor or split into parts
- **Tip**: Most phones take photos that are too large - compress first!

### Upload not working:
1. Check your internet connection
2. Try a different browser
3. Clear browser cache
4. Make sure files are correct format

### Can't see upload button:
1. Make sure you're logged in
2. Navigate to Profile tab
3. Click "Dashboard" button
4. Scroll to "Content Management" section

### Post not showing:
- If scheduled: Wait until scheduled time
- If immediate: Refresh the page
- Check the correct tab (Posts/Media/Videos)
