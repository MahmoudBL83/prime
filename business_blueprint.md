PRIME APP BLUEPRINT

0) Product Vision & Principles
Vision: Build the world's most complete learning ecosystem, where anyone can find a study partner, access a massive all access library of courses, subscribe to premium, high-production "signature" programs, or join creator-led membership channels for personalized coaching and community.

Guiding principles
Learning outcomes first: Everything ladders up to helping people learn faster, retain more, and achieve real world milestones.
Access for all: A single subscription unlocks a huge baseline of knowledge; higher tiers and creator memberships add depth and personalization.
Creators as partners: Clear policies, fair pay, great tools, and zero unnecessary friction.
Trust & safety by design: Strong moderation, contracts, and transparent rewards/contests to protect learners, creators, and the business.
Delightful UX: Low cognitive load, helpful defaults, and fast performance across devices.

1) Content Model (Three Categories)
Category A- All-Access Course Library (open contribution with guidelines)
One subscription unlocks a large library of pre-recorded courses across nearly every topic ("Amazon of Learning").
Any approved creator can publish here, provided they pass onboarding and content quality guidelines.
Revenue share is usage-based (e.g., watch time/engagement weighting).
Staff moderation before first publication for each creator; ongoing spot checks and automated QA thereafter.

Category B Signature Courses (curated, premium tier)
Separate premium subscription for high-production, expertly curated programs from established experts.
Editorial standards are much stricter (script review, learning design review, production review).
Fewer, deeper programs with cinematic video, structured workbooks, and assessments.
We curate and invite; we monitor continuously for quality and policy compliance.

Category C Creator Membership Channels (individual creator subscriptions)
Learners subscribe to specific creators monthly to access their private feed: short videos, long-form lessons, resources, discussion posts, live sessions, group Q&A, and 1:1 or cohort-based meetings.
Built for interactive coaching rather than entertainment; strictly education-focused. Creators can bundle perks (e.g., community groups, homework feedback, office hours).
Pricing set by creators within platform ranges; the platform takes a transparent fee.

2) User (Learner) Experience
2.1 Onboarding
Choose interests, skill level, goals, languages, and availability.
Optionally verify age; parental/guardian controls for minors.
Set learning mode preferences: self-paced, cohort, 1:1 coaching.
Enable Study Buddy Matching (swipe-style): set subjects, time zones, study cadence, and collaboration preferences (chat, audio, co-watch, shared notes).
Acceptance criteria
Users complete onboarding in $\le3$ minutes.
Profile completeness indicator; can skip and finish later.
Safe defaults (privacy: profile limited by default; opt-in visibility).

2.2 Study Buddy Matching (swipe-based)
Card Ul with name (or alias), subjects, goals, schedule fit, and compatibility score.
Quick actions: connect, pass, super-connect (prioritized match), report.
After a match: auto-create a shared study space (chat + calendar + resource pinboard + optional co-watch).
Smart re-matching if a buddy becomes inactive.
Matching signals
Subject/topic overlap, timezone overlap, session length preferences, goal alignment (exam vs portfolio vs career), language, headset availability, reliability score.
Safety
Report/block; auto-moderation for messages; age-appropriate matching.

2.3 Discovery & Search
Global search across courses, creators, membership channels, live events, and resources.
Filters: category (A/B/C), level, language, duration, rating, price, live vs recorded, certificate, accessibility (captions), and format (project-based, exam-prep, etc.).
Home feed curated by goals + behavior; explainability badge ("Why am I seeing this?").

2.4 Learning in Category A (All-Access Library)
Course page: overview, outcomes, syllabus, lessons, transcripts, captions, downloads (DRM-protected/offline), notes, discussions, quizzes, assignments.
Completion tracking; streaks and reminders; printable/shareable certificate (if set by creator).
Recommendations at lesson and course end.

2.5 Learning in Category B (Signature Courses)
Structured pathways: pre-assessment, modules, capstone projects, expert feedback windows.
High-quality workbooks and resources; cohort calendars.
Enhanced coach Q&A office hours (group); limited seats for intimacy.

2.6 Learning in Category C (Creator Membership Channels)
Subscribe to a creator; see their member-only feed (posts, clips, full lessons, resources).
Access to group spaces + live session links + archived recordings.
Tiered perks (e.g., 1:1 slots, feedback tokens, priority Q&A).

2.7 Rewards, Scholarships, and Giveaways
Course-level leaderboards with objective metrics (quiz scores, project rubrics, completed assignments, peer reviews, instructor verification).
Creator-funded or platform-funded prize pools (opt-in) for top learners-framed as scholarships or learning prizes, fully compliant with local laws.
Transparent rules, eligibility, and anti-fraud checks (identity verification for large prizes).
Example programs
Course Scholarship: top project wins a cash scholarship.
Exam Excellence Award: top verified score earns a high-value prize (e.g., learning equipment or a major award), subject to legal and verification steps.

2.8 Payments & Subscriptions (Learner)
One-tap subscribe (A), upgrade (B), and per-creator subscribe (C).
Wallet view of active plans, renewal dates, invoices, and refunds.
Family plan and student discounts (verification optional).

2.9 Support
Contextual help; 24/7 web ticketing; safety hotline for abuse.

3) Creator (Instructor) Experience
3.1 Creator Onboarding & Approval
Apply in-app with identity verification, expertise proof, sample content, teaching goals.
Review timeline: up to 10 days to approve/decline or request revisions (status tracking in app).
Upon approval: access Creator Manual and Contract in app; e-sign and upload tax/payout documents.

3.2 Publishing Options
Category A: Create a structured course, pass first-time content review (quality + policy), then publish to the All-Access Library.
Category B: Invitation/curation only; we provide editorial support and production guidelines.
Category C: Launch a membership channel with posts, lessons, resources, groups, chat, and live sessions; ongoing monitoring for policy compliance.

3.3 Tools & Workflows
Course Builder (A/B): syllabus planner, bulk upload, transcript & caption generator, quiz/assignment creator, workbook templates.
Membership Channel (C): post composer, content scheduling, tier setup, live events, recurring office hours, group spaces, member messaging, polls.
Community: create member groups, set rules, assign moderators, pin resources, manage applications.
Monetization: set prices (within allowed ranges), view real-time earnings, payout schedule, tax forms, and refunds.
Analytics: views, completion, retention, revenue by plan, cohort progress, top posts, churn drivers.
Asset Management: safe storage, versioning, DRM, watermarking, and optional offline availability.

3.4 Policies & Reviews
Before first publish to Category A: mandatory human review.
For Category C: continuous monitoring (automated + spot checks); creators remain responsible for educational focus and safe conduct.
For Category B: editorial partnership; content reviewed at multiple gates.

3.5 Interactions & Teaching
Integrated chat (with moderation), quizzes, submissions, rubrics, feedback videos, badges.
Scheduling: native calendar, time-zone smart slots, reminders, and attendance tracking.
Live: low-latency streaming, screen share, whiteboard, breakout rooms, recordings for members.

3.6 Earning & Payouts
Category A: revenue share from the all-access subscription based on engagement (e.g., weighted watch time + completions).
Category B: curated deals (rev share and/or fixed fees; possible minimum guarantees).
Category C: per-member monthly fees; platform fee + processing.
Bonuses: participation in scholarship/reward pools is opt-in; clear accounting and visibility.

4) Company (Admin) Perspective "Mission Control"
4.1 Admin Console
User 360: profiles, subscriptions, support tickets, risk flags.
Creator 360: onboarding pipeline, contracts, KYC status, strike history, payout status.
Content 360: review queues (pre-publish for A), monitoring dashboard for C, editorial pipeline for B, takedown tools, DMCA workflow.
Financials: revenue by product, AR, refund rates, payout batches, ledger export, tax reporting.
Rewards: configure scholarship rules, eligibility, prize inventory, selection audits, and winner verification.
Safety: abuse reports, message filters, keyword dashboards, age-gated controls, ban/appeal tooling, audit logs.
Operations: feature flags, pricing/catalog manager, promotions, localized pricing, communication templates.

4.2 Roles & Permissions
Super Admin, Content Ops, Trust & Safety, Finance Ops, Editorial, Creator Success, Support Agent, Analyst (read-only). Granular permissions by area.

4.3 Observability
Real-time metrics (signups, activations, DAU/WAU/MAU, conversion, retention, LTV/CAC, refunds, creator churn, review SLA, abuse response time).
Event lake for experimentation and ML ranking.

5) Monetization & Pricing (Draft)
Category A (All-Access): monthly/annual subscription; family plan; student/educator discounts.
Category B (Signature): separate premium subscription (monthly/annual). Option to bundle A+B at a discount.
Category C (Membership Channels): per-creator monthly fee; optional tiers (e.g., Basic, Plus, Coaching). Platform fee + payment processing.
Add-ons: certificates, priority support, cohort seats, extra 1:1 tokens (educational services only).
Payout logic (examples)
A: Creator pool $=\%$ of net revenue; distribute by weighted engagement.
B: Negotiated terms per program.
C: Net of platform + processing; pay out monthly with reserve for chargebacks.

6) Trust, Safety, and Compliance
Education-only policy for membership channels; prohibited content list in the Creator Manual.
Age safety: minor protections, verification for large prizes, age-appropriate matching.
Communication safety: Al + human moderation for chat, comments, and posts; block/report; rate limits; link controls.
Giveaways/Scholarships: clear rules, no purchase necessary where required; region-specific compliance; auditing and public winner announcements.
Copyright & IP: creator warrants originality; license grants to platform; DMCA-style takedowns; strike system.
Privacy: data minimization, regional DPA addendum, parental rights for minors, transparent data uses.
Accessibility: captions, transcripts, contrast and keyboard support, audio descriptions where feasible.

7) Learning Design Standards (Summary)
Outcomes stated up front; syllabus mapped to outcomes; assessments tied to outcomes.
Minimum audio/video specs; lighting and mic standards; pacing and chaptering.
Inclusive language; examples across cultures; alt text for visuals.
Review checklists for A (creator-submitted) and editorial rubrics for B (signature).

14) Risk Register (Selected)
Compliance risk with rewards: mitigate via jurisdictional templates and legal review.
Abuse risk in chat/live: mitigate via active moderation + rate limits + reporting.
Quality dilution in A: mitigate via guidelines, reviews, and ranking.
Refund/chargeback in C: mitigate via clear value promises, trial tools, time-boxed content guarantees.
Creator churn: mitigate via analytics, success coaching, and timely payouts.

2. Category A first-time publish requires review; Category C is monitored post-publish.
3. Rewards are framed as scholarships/prizes with full legal compliance.
4. DRM/offline allowed for paid learners per license.

Functioning Blueprint (Education Platform)
1-User & Creator Onboarding
Automated
Learner signup $\to$ email/phone verification.
Creator signup $\to$ guided form (bio, pricing, upload ID/KYC).
Stripe Connect auto-handles ID verification + bank info.
Welcome sequences (emails, in-app tutorials).
Auto-create profile page with upload tools.

2-Content Creation & Publishing
Automated
Upload video $\to$ system transcodes to standard formats.
Auto-generate captions/subtitles.
Store in secure cloud (signed URLs for access).
Tagging + categorization based on transcript.
Auto-publish to creator’s feed.
Auto-notify subscribers.

3-Content Discovery
Automated
Recommendation engine (suggest creators based on viewing/subscriptions).
Search with filters (tags, price, rating).
Trending content auto-ranked based on engagement.
“You might like” email campaigns.

4-Payments & Finance
Automated
Subscription renewal each billing cycle.
Platform fee automatically deducted.
Creator payouts processed weekly/monthly.
Dunning process: failed cards $\to$ retry $\to$ cancel if not fixed.
Auto-generated invoices & receipts.
Auto-refund small amounts (<$50, policy based).
Tax info auto-collected via Stripe.

5-Customer Support
Automated
Chatbot answers FAQs (billing, access, subscriptions).
Help Center with searchable guides.
Ticket system routes to correct category.
Auto close inactive tickets after reminders.

6-Moderation & Trust & Safety
Automated
AI filters for adult/violent/illegal content.
Spam account detection.
Auto-ban obvious rule violations.
Keyword filters in messages.
Temporary takedown of content flagged by DMCA form.

7-Marketing & Growth
Automated
Email automation (welcome, upsell, churn prevention).
Referral system: track invites, auto-credit rewards.
Creator affiliate links auto-tracked.
SEO: auto-generate blog posts from transcripts.
Social preview generator for content sharing.

8-Analytics & Reporting
Automated
Creators get dashboards:
Revenue, subs, churn, content views.
Platform admin gets dashboards:
GMV, churn, lifetime value, MRR.
Alerts when metrics spike/drop (e.g., refund > 10%).
Weekly/monthly reports emailed automatically.

9-Legal & Compliance
Automated
Auto-collect creator IDs for KYC.
Store moderation + payout logs for audits.
Block restricted countries automatically.
Temporary takedown on copyright claim.

10-Business Development
Automated
Leaderboards show fast-growing creators.
System suggests “creators to feature.”
Auto-detect enterprise leads (large groups registering together).
